Mount face and grid offset you set on a part are remembered for that file. Download them to keep a snap data file for your library.

Print plates

Each plate uses one nozzle size, so parts are grouped by the nozzle set for their part group. Defaults: tiles 0.6 mm, shelves and bins 0.8 mm, everything with a precise fit 0.4 mm. Turn on X-Y hole compensation in Bambu Studio for 0.6 and 0.8 mm plates. Parts keep the print orientation from their STL file and are packed onto as few plates as possible. Each plate exports as a .3mf you open in Bambu Studio to slice and send to the printer. Stand-in tiles and mock components are left off.
